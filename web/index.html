Security & Privacy

Last Sync: May 8, 2026

The Cyber OS architecture is built on the principle of Zero-Knowledge Privacy. This manifest outlines how your data is handled within our encrypted ecosystem.

0x01 Data Collection

We minimize data footprint to ensure maximum security. Our nodes collect:

  • Hashed Device ID: Anonymized identifier for session stability across TV/Mobile transitions.
  • Local Cache: Preferences and watch history are stored locally on your hardware, never on our central relays.
  • Telemetry: Real-time performance logs (non-PII) to maintain system integrity.

0x02 Encryption Protocols

All outbound requests for metadata and stream URI resolution are routed through encrypted tunnels. We utilize AES-256 for local storage and TLS 1.3 for all network transmissions.

0x03 Third-Party Handshakes

Our framework interacts with external APIs (Firebase, IMDb) only through sanitized proxy layers to prevent IP leakage and fingerprinting.
